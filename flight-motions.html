How to read it

The grey limbs are the far side of the body, white the near side — same depth rule the arena uses. The left ruler is altitude in rig units, where one unit is a hip-to-knee bone and a standing fighter is 1.42 tall.

Wiring one up

Every motion is a pure f(p) over a 0–1 loop returning {rig, rot, x, y}. Drop the body into a new ft.anim case in animRig and feed it 1 - ft.animT/dur.

What's still missing

Rotation is new — drawCharacter plants the rig at ground level with no body angle, so it needs an opts.rot that spins joints about the hip before projecting.
